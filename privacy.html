Privacy Policy

School System (the "app") is an Android app for school and college fee management. Last updated: 31 October 2026. Home

What the app accesses

  • Google account: your email address and name, only to sign you in and to identify you to the app owner.
  • Google Drive: only the app's own hidden app-data folder (scope drive.appdata). The app stores a backup copy of your school data there (institutes, classes, students, fees, payments and message records). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Phone permissions: internet, notifications, and SMS (only when you choose to send messages to parents).

What is sent to the app owner

  • Your name and email when you sign in.
  • When you request a plan: institute name, plan, UTR / transaction ID and the payment screenshot you choose to upload.
  • A monthly Excel report of your data, only if the owner asks and you tap Allow. You can turn this off in Settings.

How the data is used

Your data is used only to run the app: sign-in, backup and restore, verifying payments and activating your plan. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

  • Your school data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive backup until you delete it.
  • Payment screenshots are deleted automatically after about 6 months. Payment records and plan details are kept while your account is active.

Deleting your data

  • Remove the backup: Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > School System > Delete hidden app data.
  • Withdraw access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your records held by the app owner (name, email, payment and plan records), email us and we will remove them.
